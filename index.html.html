// About

Auditor, researcher, and Chartered Accountant.

Prosper Fuadedzi is a Chartered Accountant (Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana) and an Audit & Assurance Senior at Deloitte & Touche LLP in Atlanta, where he leads financial statement audits under U.S. GAAP.

He previously spent three years at KPMG in Accra leading audits of private- and public-sector clients, and began his career at the Ghana Revenue Authority. He holds a Master of Professional Accountancy from Georgia State University and a first-class Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting from the University of Cape Coast, and is a CPA candidate.

His research tackles a practical problem: auditors and oversight bodies can now access entire populations of financial, payroll, procurement, and expense data, but can review only a small fraction of it. He develops methods that rank risk across complete populations and show auditors why each item was flagged, so technology strengthens professional judgment instead of replacing it.

Risk-based audit analyticsRanking companies, contracts, and transactions by risk across complete populations.
Fraud & waste detectionFinancial-statement fraud, procurement overruns, payroll and purchase-card misuse.
